Home

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spinach or ugu leaves, chopped
  • 1/2 cup palm oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1-2 scotch bonnet peppers
  • 2 cups assorted meats or fish
  • 1/2 cup ground crayfish
  • 2 bouillon cubes
  • Salt and seasoning to taste

Instructions

  1. Blend peppers with onion and a little water until smooth.
  2. Heat palm oil in a pot, fry the onion and pepper mix for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the meats or fish, seasoning, and crayfish. Cook until tender.
  4. Stir in the chopped spinach leaves and simmer for 5-7 minutes.
  5. Adjust salt and seasoning, then serve hot with rice, yams, or fufu.
