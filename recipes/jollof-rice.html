Jollof Rice

Home

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 red bell peppers, blended
  • 2 tomatoes, blended
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon thyme
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots)
  • 1 pound chicken or protein of choice, cooked

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear and set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Sauté onions until translucent.
  3. Add tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes, then stir in blended peppers and tomatoes.
  4. Season with thyme, curry powder, bay leaves, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
  5. Pour in chicken broth and bring the sauce to a simmer.
  6. Add rice, mixed vegetables, and cooked protein. Stir once to combine.
  7. Cover the pot and cook on low heat for 20-25 minutes, or until rice is tender and liquid is absorbed.
  8. Fluff with a fork, remove bay leaves, and serve warm.
